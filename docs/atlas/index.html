The report measures the pool against its peers. This is the pool itself: every player of Czech nationality with a regular-season game in the NHL, the SHL, the Liiga, the National League, the DEL or the Extraliga since 2020/21, his games by season coloured by the rung of the league, and his seasons when you open his row.

In 2025/26, 29 Czech players had NHL games, 41 played in rung 2 and 346 in the Extraliga.

Czech nationality follows the report's one definition: citizenship where a league publishes it, then the national-team roster, then eligibility. For 648 of the 866 players the only evidence is the Extraliga foreigner flag or a licence (193 by citizenship, 25 by a national-team roster). Age is the season start year minus the birth year. Not covered: the KHL (by design), the AHL (its terms), the Czech 1st league and every other league; a season there is absent here, not zero. Sources and access dates are on the report page.
