Czech hockey: the player pool

How deep is the Czech professional pool, measured against nine peer nations · 2025/26

1.83* Czech NHL players per million inhabitants, 2025/26

Per head, Czechia ranks 4th of 10 nations in the NHL and 5th in the NHL plus the four strongest European leagues. The Czech NHL group peaked at 58 players in 2001/02; it is 20 now.

Three minutes: this number and the six findings below. The rest is the evidence.

* 20 Czech players with at least 20 of 82 NHL games (pro-rated to the schedule) in 2025/26, per 10.9 million inhabitants (Eurostat); every peer counted the same way. Methodology.

What to take from it

1

Per head, the Czech pool sits above the peer median, not at the bottom: 4th of 10 in the NHL.

20 Czech players reached the games threshold in the NHL in 2025/26, 1.83 per million inhabitants against a peer median of 1.21. Counting the four strongest European leagues as well, 53 players, 4.86 per million, 5th of 10.

Sweden and Finland are in another class: 7.26 and 7.25 NHL players per million, about 4 times the Czech rate.

2

The Czech NHL group fell from 58 players in 2001/02 to 20 in 2025/26 — gradually, not in one break.

The most likely season for a fall is 2009/10, with only 9% probability; the step is ×0.83 (90% interval 0.56–1.14). The fitted level is now 43% of its 2002/03 peak.

Finland and Sweden show only upward steps over the same seasons.

3

The gap is young: no Czech skater aged 21 or under reaches the games threshold in the NHL or rung 2.

The largest shortfall against the peer median is forwards aged 21 or under: 6.1 players, then forwards aged 22–25 (2.8).

4

At home, under-21 skaters get 7.9% of Extraliga games; the Liiga gives its young 14.3% and the SHL 16.7%.

On ice time the gap is wider: 5.5% against 11.9% and 11.4%, averaged over 2014/15–2025/26. In 2025/26 the Extraliga share was 5.7% of games.

5

Abroad, Czech skaters in the NHL play the median ice time for their position and score below it; in the Liiga and the SHL they are above the median on both.

Over 2020/21–2025/26, Czech NHL skaters played 0.98 times the median ice time of their position and scored 0.87 times its points per game. In the Liiga the ratios are 1.09 and 1.40, in the SHL 1.08 and 1.30.

6

Since 2010 the national team has drawn on the NHL (30%), the Extraliga (28%) and the KHL (25%) in about equal parts; rung 2 gives 13%.

Averaged over 21 World Championship and Olympic rosters. The KHL is outside the atlas's leagues, so 25% of the roster spots come from a league the atlas does not measure.

What is counted

Ten nations, six leagues, one definition of nationality, and three leagues and one database left out on purpose.

The ladder

Rung 1
NHL.
Rung 2
SHL (Sweden), Liiga (Finland), National League (Switzerland), DEL (Germany). "NHL + rung 2" is the headline's five leagues.
Home
The Czech Extraliga. For Finland, Sweden, Switzerland and Germany the home league is already rung 2.
Other
Everything else in the data.

Nations: Czechia against Finland, Sweden, Switzerland, Slovakia, Germany, Latvia, Denmark, Norway, Austria. Population: Eurostat, 1 January of the season's second year.

Nationality, one definition

A player's nationality is taken, in this order, from:

  1. citizenship where a league publishes it: the NHL's nationalityCode, the Liiga's and the SHL's nationality (the SHL's is mostly "N/A" before about 2010);
  2. the national-team roster at a World Championship or Olympics since 2010 (IIHF eligibility);
  3. eligibility or licence where that is all a source has: the Extraliga's foreigner flag, the DEL's licence nationality (from 2022/23), the National League's Swiss licence.

Players are linked across leagues on the folded name plus the birth date, so a Czech in the SHL is recognised from his NHL or Extraliga record. Czechoslovak-era births take the present-day state the NHL recodes them to. Of the 867 players on the pool page, 649 are Czech on eligibility alone.

Left out

  • KHL, by design. It removes a real destination, especially before 2014, and it is where 25% of the Czech national team's roster spots since 2010 came from.
  • AHL: its terms forbid automated access. AHL seasons appear only in NHL players' career records, a survivor-biased view.
  • Slovak Extraliga: its robots.txt blocks automated access. Slovakia is measured through its players in the covered leagues.
  • Elite Prospects: its terms forbid scraping.

Question 1

Is the Czech pool thin?

Not per head. Czechia ranks 4th of 10 for NHL players at 1.83 per million, above the peer median of 1.21; Sweden leads at 7.26.

How we know

What we did For each nation and season we counted the distinct players of that nationality who reached the games threshold in the NHL (or in any of the five leagues), then divided by the population. The threshold is at least 20 of 82 games, pro-rated to the league-season's schedule (95th percentile of skater games played): 20 games in a full NHL season, fewer in the short 2012/13 and 2020/21 seasons and in shorter European schedules.

Sensitivity Counting any game at all, Czechia has 29 NHL players (2.66 per million, 4th) and 69 in the five leagues (5th). With a flat 20 games everywhere, 20 and 53 (5th).

Coverage In 2025/26 every qualifying NHL, Liiga and DEL player has a nationality; the National League has 22 of 365 unidentified (foreign licences with no match), the SHL 4 of 365. Only 4 seasons of the rung 1 + 2 series (2022/23–2025/26) have every rung-2 league at least 90% identified.

Question 2

When did it break?

It did not break in one season: it wore down. The most likely fall is 2009/10, with 9% probability; the fitted level is now 43% of its 2002/03 peak.

How we know

The model A Poisson local-level model, the one the football atlas uses for its series: the log of the expected count follows a random walk plus two step changes, and the step seasons are marginalised over every allowed pair (at least three seasons from the ends and from each other). 2004/05, the lockout, stays in the time index with no likelihood. Priors: σ ~ HalfNormal(0.2), step ~ Normal(0, 1).

The Czech fall The step is ×0.83 (90% interval 0.56–1.14); the probability that it lowers the level is 0.80. The earlier rise is dated to 1998/99. The fitted level peaked at about 53 players in 2002/03 and is 23 in 2025/26.

Diagnostics 4 chains × 1000 draws after 1000 tuning, seed 42; largest R-hat 1.007, 1 divergent transition over the three fits. There is no forecast: the atlas is descriptive.

Question 3

Where is it thin?

In the youngest band: no Czech skater aged 21 or under reaches the games threshold in the NHL or rung 2 in 2025/26. The largest shortfall is forwards aged 21 or under: 6.1 players below the peer median.

How we know

What we did Qualifying players in the NHL and rung 2 in 2025/26, counted once per person, split by position and by age (season start year minus birth year). The shortfall is the gap to the peer median per million times the Czech population (10.9 million): the number of players Czechia would need to reach the median.

Largest shortfalls forwards ≤21: 6.1; forwards 22–25: 2.8; defencemen ≤21: 2.0; forwards 30+: 0.9; defencemen 26–29: 0.0.

Question 4

Do young players get ice time at home?

Less than in Finland and Sweden. Under-21 skaters play 7.9% of Extraliga games and 5.5% of its ice time; the Liiga gives them 14.3% and 11.9%, the SHL 16.7% and 11.4%.

How we know

What we did Every skater of every nationality, regular season. A league-season counts as measured when ages are known for at least 90% of its skater games; the Extraliga, Liiga and SHL are measured in all 12 seasons. Ice time is published by the Extraliga from 2013/14, the Liiga from 2014/15 and the SHL from 2009/10. Goalkeepers are in question 7.

Ratios The Extraliga's under-21 share of games is 0.56 times the Liiga's and 0.48 times the SHL's; of ice time, 0.46 and 0.48. In 2025/26 the Extraliga share was 5.7% of games and 4.1% of ice time.

Question 5

How do they fare abroad?

In the NHL, Czech skaters play 0.98 times the median ice time of their position and score 0.87 times its points per game. In the Liiga (1.09, 1.40) and the SHL (1.08, 1.30) they are above the median on both counts.

How we know

What we did Qualifying skaters (the games threshold) over 2020/21–2025/26; each player-season is divided by the median of all qualifying skaters of the same league, season and position (forward or defenceman), and the table shows the median of those ratios. 222 Czech player-seasons in all; 3 have no position and are left out.

By position NHL forwards 1.00 (ice time) and 1.01 (points), NHL defencemen 0.91 and 0.83; Liiga forwards 1.53 on points, SHL forwards 1.50.

Question 6

What is the national team built from?

Since 2010, Czech World Championship and Olympic rosters have been on average 30% NHL, 28% Extraliga, 25% KHL and 13% rung 2.

How we know

What we did Rosters from the English Wikipedia articles for each World Championship and Olympic tournament since 2010 (CC BY-SA 4.0), each player linked to his league records. A player's club is placed in a league by these rules in order: the league Wikipedia gives, a list of KHL clubs, his NHL career record for that season, his own league row, a team-name match, then the club's country.

Placement nhl career 1,367, league row 1,356, club country 625, khl club 565, club league 559, team name 249, unknown 128 roster rows (all nations). The Czech NHL share ranks 2nd of the ten nations.

Question 7

Goalkeepers

6 Czech goalkeepers reached the threshold in the NHL in 2025/26 and 11 in the NHL plus rung 2: 1.01 per million, 5th of 10, against a peer median of 0.53.

How we know

Abroad Czech goalkeepers' save percentage against the league-season median, 2020/21–2025/26: NHL -0.3 points (8 goalkeepers), Liiga +1.1 (7), SHL -0.6 (3). Small numbers: read them as descriptive.

Young goalkeepers at home Goalkeepers aged 23 or less play 21.0% of Extraliga goalkeeper games, against 37.4% in the Liiga and 20.9% in the SHL (2014/15–2025/26). Czech goalkeepers played 86% of Extraliga goalkeeper games in 2025/26.

National team Of 63 goalkeeper roster spots since 2010, 19 went to NHL goalkeepers, 22 to KHL goalkeepers, 14 to Extraliga goalkeepers and 6 to rung 2.

Definitions

Games threshold
at least 20 of 82 games, pro-rated to the league-season's schedule (95th percentile of skater games played). The headline also reports any game and a flat 20.
Age
Season start year minus birth year; under 21 means 20 or less.
Population
Eurostat demo_gind, 1 January of the season's second year.
Complete season
A rung 1 + 2 season counts as complete when every rung-2 league has at least 90% of its qualifying players' nationality identified.
Season
Regular season only, every league.

Reproducibility

make restore-snapshot puts the committed snapshot in place, make verify-snapshot checks its hashes, make analysis recomputes outputs/ (about six minutes, mostly the break model) and make pages renders this page and the pool page from them. Each output file records the SHA-256 of the snapshot files it was built from, and the tests fail when the snapshot changes without the outputs being rebuilt. Every number in this text is read from outputs/ at render time.

Limitations

  • KHL Excluded by design. It is where 25% of Czech national-team roster spots since 2010 came from, so the pool counted here is smaller than the pool the national team drew on, most of all before 2014 and before 2022.
  • AHL Not fetched (terms). An AHL season appears only when the player also has an NHL career record, which favours those who made it.
  • Birth dates The National League and the DEL publish none, so their players' ages come only from a link to another league. That removes Switzerland and Germany from the age medians in question 3 and leaves the NL and DEL youth shares in question 4 unmeasured.
  • Nationality For 649 of the 867 Czech players on the pool page, the only evidence is the Extraliga foreigner flag or a licence, which is eligibility, not citizenship. The DEL published no nationality before 2022/23, so the rung 1 + 2 series before 2022/23 is a lower bound.
  • Name-only links Records without a birth date are linked on the name alone, only when one candidate exists within four seasons; ambiguous and unmatched counts are in the table above.
  • Population A per-million rate compares nations of very different sizes; a handful of players moves a small nation's rate a lot.
  • Descriptive only Nothing here predicts a season, rates a player beyond his published statistics or advises selection.
