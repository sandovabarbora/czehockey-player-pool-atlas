Czech hockey news: a new national-team head coach and an NHL club captaincy, 29 September 2026

Atlas data as of 29 September 2026; news as of 29 September 2026

This note records two dated items of news, with their sources listed at the end. The numbers beside them are the atlas's own, from its snapshot, which this note does not refresh; the note makes no statistical claim.

A new head coach of the national team

Zdeněk Moták succeeds Radim Rulík as head coach of the Czech men's national team. The executive board of the Czech Ice Hockey Association approved the appointment on 16 April 2026, with two-year contracts.1 The staff: Zdeněk Moták with coaches Pavel Gross and Richard Král, goalkeeping coach Jaroslav Kameš, and Patrik Eliáš as general manager in place of Jiří Šlégr.1,2 The first game under the new staff is on 5 November 2026 against Sweden at the O2 Arena, Prague (Euro Hockey Tour).2

A club captaincy in the NHL

On 27 September 2026 the Boston Bruins named David Pastrňák the 28th captain in the club's history.3,4 The previous captain was Brad Marchand (captain 2023–25).3 According to hokej.cz, Pastrňák is the sixth Czech to be a permanent captain in the NHL.5 The captaincy is with his NHL club, not a national-team role.
