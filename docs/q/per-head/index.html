Question 1

Is the Czech pool thin?

Not per head. Czechia ranks 4th of 10 for NHL players at 1.83 per million, above the peer median of 1.21; Sweden leads at 7.26.

How we know

What we did For each nation and season we counted the distinct players of that nationality who reached the games threshold in the NHL (or in any of the five leagues), then divided by the population. The threshold is at least 20 of 82 games, pro-rated to the league-season's schedule (95th percentile of skater games played): 20 games in a full NHL season, fewer in the short 2012/13 and 2020/21 seasons and in shorter European schedules.

Sensitivity Counting any game at all, Czechia has 29 NHL players (2.66 per million, 4th) and 69 in the five leagues (5th). With a flat 20 games everywhere, 20 and 53 (5th).

Coverage In 2025/26 every qualifying NHL, Liiga and DEL player has a nationality; the National League has 22 of 365 unidentified (foreign licences with no match), the SHL 4 of 365. Only 4 seasons of the rung 1 + 2 series (2022/23–2025/26) have every rung-2 league at least 90% identified.

What this does not show

  • KHL Excluded by design. It is where 25% of Czech national-team roster spots since 2010 came from, so the pool counted here is smaller than the pool the national team drew on, most of all before 2014 and before 2022.
  • Nationality For 649 of the 867 Czech players on the pool page, the only evidence is the Extraliga foreigner flag or a licence, which is eligibility, not citizenship. The DEL published no nationality before 2022/23, so the rung 1 + 2 series before 2022/23 is a lower bound.
  • Population A per-million rate compares nations of very different sizes; a handful of players moves a small nation's rate a lot.
