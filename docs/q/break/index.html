Question 2

When did it break?

It did not break in one season: it wore down. The most likely fall is 2009/10, with 9% probability; the fitted level is now 43% of its 2002/03 peak.

How we know

The model A Poisson local-level model, the one the football atlas uses for its series: the log of the expected count follows a random walk plus two step changes, and the step seasons are marginalised over every allowed pair (at least three seasons from the ends and from each other). 2004/05, the lockout, stays in the time index with no likelihood. Priors: σ ~ HalfNormal(0.2), step ~ Normal(0, 1).

The Czech fall The step is ×0.83 (90% interval 0.56–1.14); the probability that it lowers the level is 0.80. The earlier rise is dated to 1998/99. The fitted level peaked at about 53 players in 2002/03 and is 23 in 2025/26.

Diagnostics 4 chains × 1000 draws after 1000 tuning, seed 42; largest R-hat 1.007, 1 divergent transition over the three fits. There is no forecast: the atlas is descriptive.

What this does not show

  • KHL Excluded by design. It is where 25% of Czech national-team roster spots since 2010 came from, so the pool counted here is smaller than the pool the national team drew on, most of all before 2014 and before 2022.
  • Population A per-million rate compares nations of very different sizes; a handful of players moves a small nation's rate a lot.
  • Descriptive only Nothing here predicts a season, rates a player beyond his published statistics or advises selection.
