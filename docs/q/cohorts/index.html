Question 3 · Czech hockey atlas · 2025/26

Czech NHL and rung-2 players by position and age against the peer median, 2025/26

In 2025/26 no Czech forward or defenceman aged 21 or under reached the games threshold in the NHL or rung 2, and the largest difference from the median of 7 peers, expressed in players at the Czech population, is 6.1 for forwards aged 21 or under.

Evidence

What was counted Qualifying players in the NHL and rung 2 in 2025/26, each counted once, split by position and by age (season start year minus birth year). The difference from the median is the gap between the peer median per million and the Czech rate, multiplied by the Czech population (10.9 million), so it is expressed in players. The peer range is the lowest and highest rate among the 7 peers in the median; the counts are the whole record for the season and have no sampling interval.

Largest differences forwards ≤21: 6.1; forwards 22–25: 2.8; defencemen ≤21: 2.0; forwards 30+: 0.9.

A positive difference means fewer Czech players than the peer median. In seasons where Switzerland or Germany has more players of known age, the number of peers in the median changes.

What this means

In 2025/26 the Czech count is furthest below the peer median among the youngest forwards. The season-by-season table shows how much one season moves: Czech forwards aged 21 or under who reached the threshold numbered 5, 3, 2, 0, 4, 0 from 2020/21 to 2025/26, and the count was at or above the peer median in 2 of those 6 seasons. The table describes where the Czech count is below other nations of its size in one season. It does not show why, whether the gap persists, or what number of players would be appropriate.

What this does not show

  • Whether young Czech players are developing more slowly; the counts only show who had reached these leagues by this age.
  • A single season is a noisy cross-section for counts of a few players. More
  • The National League and the DEL publish no birth dates, so their ages rest on links to other leagues. More
  • Each question uses its own age cut-off, stated on the page. More
  • Some nationalities rest on eligibility or licence, not citizenship. More
