Question 4 · Czech hockey atlas · 2014/15–2025/26

Share of league games and ice time played by skaters aged 20 or under, 2014/15–2025/26

Over 2014/15–2025/26, skaters aged 20 or under played 7.9 % of Extraliga skater games (95 % bootstrap interval by season 7.0 %–8.8 %), against 14.3 % (12.9 %–15.8 %) in the Liiga and 16.7 % (15.5 %–17.9 %) in the SHL.

Evidence

What was counted Every skater of every nationality, regular season. A league-season counts as measured when ages are known for at least 90 % of its skater games; the Extraliga, Liiga and SHL are measured in all 12 seasons. Ice time is published by the Extraliga from 2013/14, the Liiga from 2014/15 and the SHL from 2009/10. The cut-off here is 20 or under (under-21 hockey); question 3 uses 21 or under and the goalkeepers in question 7 use 23 or under.

Ratios The Extraliga's share of games is 0.56 times the Liiga's (95 % bootstrap interval 0.50–0.63) and 0.48 times the SHL's (0.44–0.51); of ice time, 0.46 (0.41–0.51) and 0.48 (0.44–0.53). The same seasons are resampled for both leagues. In 2025/26 the Extraliga share was 5.7 % of games and 4.1 % of ice time.

What this means

Across twelve seasons, skaters aged 20 or under took a smaller share of games and of ice time in the Extraliga than in the Liiga or the SHL, and the 95 % intervals do not overlap. On ice time, the Extraliga's highest season (7.8 %, 2020/21) was below the lowest season of both other leagues; on games, its highest season (10.5 %) was above the Liiga's lowest (10.2 %). The comparison is between league-level shares. It does not measure the opportunity a given young player receives, and the leagues differ in their share of imported players and in how often young players are loaned to lower leagues, which this measure does not separate.

What this does not show

  • Whether the young players who do not appear in these leagues are playing elsewhere (lower leagues, junior leagues, North America).
  • The National League and the DEL publish no birth dates, so their ages rest on links to other leagues. More
