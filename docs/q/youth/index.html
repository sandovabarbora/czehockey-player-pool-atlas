Question 4

Do young players get ice time at home?

Less than in Finland and Sweden. Under-21 skaters play 7.9% of Extraliga games and 5.5% of its ice time; the Liiga gives them 14.3% and 11.9%, the SHL 16.7% and 11.4%.

How we know

What we did Every skater of every nationality, regular season. A league-season counts as measured when ages are known for at least 90% of its skater games; the Extraliga, Liiga and SHL are measured in all 12 seasons. Ice time is published by the Extraliga from 2013/14, the Liiga from 2014/15 and the SHL from 2009/10. Goalkeepers are in question 7.

Ratios The Extraliga's under-21 share of games is 0.56 times the Liiga's and 0.48 times the SHL's; of ice time, 0.46 and 0.48. In 2025/26 the Extraliga share was 5.7% of games and 4.1% of ice time.

What this does not show

  • Birth dates The National League and the DEL publish none, so their players' ages come only from a link to another league. That removes Switzerland and Germany from the age medians in question 3 and leaves the NL and DEL youth shares in question 4 unmeasured.
