League composition of Czech national-team rosters, 2010–2026

Across 21 World Championship and Olympic rosters from 2010 to 2026, Czech roster spots came on average 31 % from NHL clubs, 28 % from the Extraliga, 25 % from the KHL and 13 % from rung 2, with the KHL share at 33 % over 2010–2022 and 0 % over 2023–2026.

What was counted Rosters from the English Wikipedia articles for each World Championship and Olympic tournament since 2010, each player linked to his league records. A player's club is placed in a league by these rules in order: the league Wikipedia gives, a list of KHL clubs, his NHL career record for that season, his own league row, a team-name match, then the club's country. The shares are means over tournaments of each roster's shares; they are counts of whole rosters and carry no sampling interval.

Placement of the Czech rows Of the 529 Czech roster rows, 233 were placed by the NHL career record, 110 by the player's own league row, 106 by the KHL club list, 73 by the league Wikipedia gives, 5 by the club's country, 2 by a team-name match; 0 could not be placed. The eight Czech rows that were placed by the two weakest rules (the club's country or a team-name match) before this version were checked by hand: one was an NHL player at the 2026 Olympics whose roster birth date differed from his league record, so he was not linked; the linking rule was extended and he is now placed in the NHL (see the change log). The other seven follow the club the roster article gives. Across all ten nations: nhl career 1 370, league row 1 357, club country 623, khl club 565, club league 559, team name 247, unknown 128 roster rows. The Czech NHL share ranks 2nd of the ten nations.

What this means

Czech rosters since 2010 have drawn on NHL clubs, the Extraliga and, until 2022, the KHL. From 2023 the KHL share is 0 % and the Extraliga share 43 %, against 23 % before. The composition of a roster follows from who is available and who is nominated, as well as from where Czech players play; the change after 2022 is a change in the rosters and does not by itself show a change in the pool.

What this does not show

  • Why a player was or was not nominated; the atlas records rosters, not selection decisions.
  • Players' form or role at the tournament.
  • The KHL is excluded, although it supplied a quarter of Czech national-team roster spots. More
  • Descriptive and exploratory: no cause, forecast or selection advice. More
