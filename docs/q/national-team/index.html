Question 6

What is the national team built from?

Since 2010, Czech World Championship and Olympic rosters have been on average 30% NHL, 28% Extraliga, 25% KHL and 13% rung 2.

How we know

What we did Rosters from the English Wikipedia articles for each World Championship and Olympic tournament since 2010 (CC BY-SA 4.0), each player linked to his league records. A player's club is placed in a league by these rules in order: the league Wikipedia gives, a list of KHL clubs, his NHL career record for that season, his own league row, a team-name match, then the club's country.

Placement nhl career 1,367, league row 1,356, club country 625, khl club 565, club league 559, team name 249, unknown 128 roster rows (all nations). The Czech NHL share ranks 2nd of the ten nations.

What this does not show

  • KHL Excluded by design. It is where 25% of Czech national-team roster spots since 2010 came from, so the pool counted here is smaller than the pool the national team drew on, most of all before 2014 and before 2022.
