Question 5 · Czech hockey atlas · 2020/21–2025/26

Ice time and points per game of Czech skaters against the league median, 2020/21–2025/26

Over 2020/21–2025/26, the median Czech NHL skater played 0.98 times the median ice time of his league, season and position (95 % bootstrap interval by player 0.90–1.11) and scored 0.87 times its points per game (0.68–1.42; 35 players, 111 player-seasons).

Evidence

What was counted Qualifying skaters (the games threshold) over 2020/21–2025/26; each player-season is divided by the median of all qualifying skaters of the same league, season and position (forward or defenceman), and the table shows the median of those ratios. 222 Czech player-seasons in all; 3 have no position and are left out. Only players who reached the threshold are included, so players who went abroad and did not reach it are not in the ratios.

By position NHL forwards (26 players): 1.00 on ice time (0.91–1.15) and 1.01 on points (0.63–1.65). NHL defencemen (9 players): 0.91 (0.76–1.15) and 0.83 (0.56–1.71). The NHL figure for all skaters mixes these two groups, so the share of defencemen moves it. Liiga forwards (19 players): 1.53 on points (1.35–1.80); SHL forwards (14 players): 1.50 (1.06–1.71). All intervals are 95 % bootstrap intervals by player.

What this means

In the NHL, the median Czech skater's ice time and scoring are close to the league median for his position, and both intervals include 1. In the Liiga and the SHL the Czech median ratios are above 1 (the SHL intervals reach below 1), but there Czech players are imports compared with each league's own depth players, so the ratios are not comparable across leagues and are not adjusted for league strength. The ratios describe the players who reached the threshold abroad; they do not show how Czech players who stayed at home, or who did not reach the threshold, would have fared.

What this does not show

  • A league-quality adjustment: a ratio of 1.3 in the SHL is not a better result than 1.0 in the NHL.
  • Players who did not reach the games threshold abroad (selection).
  • The AHL is not covered; AHL seasons appear only for players with an NHL record. More
  • The KHL is excluded, although it supplied a quarter of Czech national-team roster spots. More
  • Descriptive and exploratory: no cause, forecast or selection advice. More
