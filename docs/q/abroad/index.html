Question 5

How do they fare abroad?

In the NHL, Czech skaters play 0.98 times the median ice time of their position and score 0.87 times its points per game. In the Liiga (1.09, 1.40) and the SHL (1.08, 1.30) they are above the median on both counts.

How we know

What we did Qualifying skaters (the games threshold) over 2020/21–2025/26; each player-season is divided by the median of all qualifying skaters of the same league, season and position (forward or defenceman), and the table shows the median of those ratios. 222 Czech player-seasons in all; 3 have no position and are left out.

By position NHL forwards 1.00 (ice time) and 1.01 (points), NHL defencemen 0.91 and 0.83; Liiga forwards 1.53 on points, SHL forwards 1.50.

What this does not show

  • KHL Excluded by design. It is where 25% of Czech national-team roster spots since 2010 came from, so the pool counted here is smaller than the pool the national team drew on, most of all before 2014 and before 2022.
  • AHL Not fetched (terms). An AHL season appears only when the player also has an NHL career record, which favours those who made it.
  • Descriptive only Nothing here predicts a season, rates a player beyond his published statistics or advises selection.
