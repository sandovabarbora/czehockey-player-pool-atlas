Czech goalkeepers: counts per million, save percentage abroad and national-team spots

In 2025/26, 11 Czech goalkeepers reached the games threshold in the NHL or rung 2, 1.01 per million, 5th of 10 against a peer median of 0.53, and 6 of them played in the NHL.

Abroad Every league with a Czech goalkeeper who reached the threshold is shown. An interval is given only where at least five goalkeepers contribute; with two or three goalkeepers the median is a description of those few players. Save percentage is saves divided by saves plus goals against, and the difference is in percentage points (pp).

Young goalkeepers at home Goalkeepers aged 23 or under (a wider band than the skaters' 20 or under in question 4, set for goalkeepers in this atlas; the 20-or-under shares are in outputs/q7_goalkeepers.json) play 21.0 % of Extraliga goalkeeper games, against 37.4 % in the Liiga and 20.9 % in the SHL (unweighted means of seasons, 2014/15–2025/26). Czech goalkeepers played 86 % of Extraliga goalkeeper games in 2025/26.

National team Of 63 goalkeeper roster spots since 2010, 19 went to NHL goalkeepers, 22 to KHL goalkeepers, 14 to Extraliga goalkeepers, 6 to rung 2 and 2 to other leagues.

What this means

Relative to population, Czechia had more goalkeepers in the NHL and rung 2 in 2025/26 than the median peer, from counts of a few goalkeepers per nation. Abroad, the Czech goalkeepers' median save percentages differ from their league medians by between −0.6 and +1.4 percentage points, from at most 8 goalkeepers per league. These are descriptions of small groups of named players; they do not rank goalkeepers or nations by quality.

What this does not show

  • Goalkeeper quality adjusted for shot quality or team defence; save percentage is the only measure used.
  • The AHL is not covered; AHL seasons appear only for players with an NHL record. More
  • Each question uses its own age cut-off, stated on the page. More
  • The denominator is total population, not registered players. More
