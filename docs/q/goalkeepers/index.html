Question 7

Goalkeepers

6 Czech goalkeepers reached the threshold in the NHL in 2025/26 and 11 in the NHL plus rung 2: 1.01 per million, 5th of 10, against a peer median of 0.53.

How we know

Abroad Czech goalkeepers' save percentage against the league-season median, 2020/21–2025/26: NHL -0.3 points (8 goalkeepers), Liiga +1.1 (7), SHL -0.6 (3). Small numbers: read them as descriptive.

Young goalkeepers at home Goalkeepers aged 23 or less play 21.0% of Extraliga goalkeeper games, against 37.4% in the Liiga and 20.9% in the SHL (2014/15–2025/26). Czech goalkeepers played 86% of Extraliga goalkeeper games in 2025/26.

National team Of 63 goalkeeper roster spots since 2010, 19 went to NHL goalkeepers, 22 to KHL goalkeepers, 14 to Extraliga goalkeepers and 6 to rung 2.

What this does not show

  • AHL Not fetched (terms). An AHL season appears only when the player also has an NHL career record, which favours those who made it.
  • Population A per-million rate compares nations of very different sizes; a handful of players moves a small nation's rate a lot.
  • Descriptive only Nothing here predicts a season, rates a player beyond his published statistics or advises selection.
